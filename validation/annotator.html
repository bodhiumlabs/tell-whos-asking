Cues

community (c): the host, not whether it sounds human: forum/board, comments, Q&A (reddit, StackExchange), mailing list → y; an article / interview / blog post → n; a reader comment or reply → y.

templated (t): a canned/boilerplate unit (FAQ/PAA answer, SEO/marketing question, one of a mass-published list). Generic wording ≠ templated: a reader's forum/comment post is n even if plainly worded. If community=y, templated is almost always n. Bespoke interview / op-ed / rhetorical → n.

personal (p): first-person, or about the asker's own situation ("how do I fix my 2012 Civic?") → y; otherwise n.

Labels

provenance: where the question lives:
genuine-UGC: forum/board, comment thread, Q&A community, mailing list
manufactured-template: a canonical question duplicated across many sites (FAQ/PAA/SEO)
commerce-faq: a shop or support/FAQ site's own Q&A
editorial: news / blog / magazine / article / interview content
unknown: can't place it.

type: grammatical form:
wh: who/what/when/where/why/which/how
polar: yes/no, auxiliary-first ("Is it…?", "Do you…?")
tag: statement + tag ("…, right?", "…, isn't it?")
imperative-interrogative: a command-style request ("tell me…", "explain…")
elliptical: no verb ("best way to X?", "reason for Y?")
other: none of these.

person: first (I / we) · second (you) · impersonal (neither).

scaffolding: y = extra context around the question: more than one sentence, a background clause, or politeness ("I have a 2012 Civic that won't start. How do I fix it?"); n = a bare question.
